## Notes on use

SyncForge copies, overwrites and deletes files according to your profiles. In particular, the "Mirror" and "Two-way" modes can delete files. Use the preview before the first run, use safety features such as deletion protection, the Recycle Bin or "Versioned" mode, and keep additional, independent backups of particularly important data.
